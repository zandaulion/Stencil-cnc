Bridge settings

MinimalSecure
OrderlyOrganic

Minimal chooses the shortest connector tree. Aesthetic also follows the active filter and avoids important detail. Follow features searches for dark, locally aligned positions where added metal is less visible, and may use one extra sparse slat station to route around a face. Secure adds a small number of separated backup ties. Slat stabilizers include kerf allowance and the completed geometry is checked after kerf. Their span target is a guide, not a strength certification; confirm it for the stock, thickness, mounting, and cut sequence. Orange dashed ties are safe fallbacks to review.
